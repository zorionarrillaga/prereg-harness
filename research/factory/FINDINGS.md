### F002 · Pointed at a 12-day synthetic pair, the not-blind gate detects the planted edge (0.5375, CI [0.5195, 0.5579]) and declines to certify four of the seven checks for want of power, printing achieved MDEs of 0.0193-0.0481 percentile points.

`measured` · 2026-08-21 · tags: notblind, power, equivalence

The null form is EQUIVALENCE — the whole interval must lie inside a band scaled to the measured lift — not 'does the CI span 0.5'. The span form is satisfied by a WIDE interval, so it gets EASIER to pass with less data; the equivalence form gets harder, which is the difference between a validator and a rubber stamp. At 12 days the intervals are wider than the band, so the gate reports INSUFFICIENT POWER and names the effect size it could have seen. Raise --days and the same checks become answerable.

**Reproduce:** `python3 bin/factory_notblind.py gate --days 12 --seed 7`

**Evidence:** `bin/factory_notblind.py` · `research/factory/registry.jsonl`
